# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. Describe behavior and evidence when disagreeing. Make room for different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not include private music, document identifiers, or personal data in bug reports without permission.

The repository owner moderates project spaces and may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or block accounts to stop harmful behavior. Give a factual explanation when moderation can be discussed without exposing private information.

Report conduct problems to the repository owner through a private channel when one is available. Otherwise request private contact without posting the incident's sensitive details. Reports must be handled with care and without retaliation.
